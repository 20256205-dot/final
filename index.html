Trade skills, not money

SkillSwap Baguio helps people in Baguio share what they know and learn what they need. One hour of help is worth one hour back.

Browse skills See how it works

Four ways to swap

  • Tutoring and School Help

    Math, science, writing and study skills.

  • Tech Skills

    Computer basics, spreadsheets and safe social media.

  • Home, Garden and Repair

    Gardening, cooking, sewing and simple repairs.

  • Arts, Music and Culture

    Guitar, drawing, weaving and local languages.

Why this website?

Many students and families in Baguio cannot pay for tutoring or lessons. This project supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education, by helping people learn from one another for free.
