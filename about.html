About SkillSwap Baguio

This website is a school project for UN Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education. It is about good learning for everyone.

In Baguio, many students and families cannot pay for tutoring or lessons. SkillSwap Baguio lets people trade skills instead of money.

Who is it for?

  • College students who have skills to share
  • Families who need help with learning or daily tasks
  • Older people who want to learn or teach

The time idea

One hour of help is worth one hour back. This is called time banking. It does not matter what the skill is.
