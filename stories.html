Success Stories

See how swapping skills could help people in Baguio.

Note: These stories are made-up examples for this school project. They are not real people.

  • Ana and the math reviewer

    Ana, a college student, helped a Grade 9 learner with math. In return, the family taught her how to start a small vegetable garden.

  • Mang Ben learns his phone

    Mang Ben wanted to video call his grandchildren. A student taught him for two hours. He taught her basic woodworking.

  • Lisa and the guitar

    Lisa traded graphic design help for guitar lessons, and made posters for a school event with her new friend.
