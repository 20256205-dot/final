Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Do I need money to join?

No. SkillSwap only trades time and skills. Please do not exchange money.

What is time banking?

One hour of help is worth one hour back. It does not matter what the skill is.

Who can join?

Students, families and older people in Baguio. Young learners need a parent or guardian.

Is this a real service?

No. It is a school project for UN SDG 4. The listings are samples and the forms do not send anything.

Where should we meet?

Choose a public place such as a school, library or community center. See the safety tips.

Still have a question? Contact us.
