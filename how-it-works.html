How It Works

Swapping skills takes four simple steps. No money is needed.

  1. Browse. Look through the skill groups and find something you want to learn.
  2. Offer or request. Use the offer form or the request form.
  3. Agree on a time. Choose a safe public place and a time that works for both people.
  4. Swap. Teach for one hour, then learn for one hour.

The time idea

This is called time banking. One hour of help is worth one hour back, no matter the skill. An hour of math tutoring can be traded for an hour of gardening help.
