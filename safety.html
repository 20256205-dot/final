Staying Safe

Safe swaps make a strong community. Please follow these tips every time.

Before you meet

  • Meet in a public place such as a school, library or community center.
  • Tell a family member or friend where you are going and when.
  • Keep personal details, like your home address, private until you trust the person.

During the swap

  • Do not exchange money. SkillSwap is only for trading time and skills.
  • Stop the session if you feel uncomfortable. You can always leave.
  • Young learners should have a parent or guardian nearby.

If something goes wrong

Tell a trusted adult or your school right away. You can also send us a message on the contact page.
